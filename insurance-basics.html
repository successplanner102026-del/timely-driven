INSURANCE · COVERAGE BASICS

What to compare before getting auto insurance quotes

Compare more than the headline price. The details of coverage, deductibles, exclusions, and service can matter just as much.

Important

This guide is general education, not insurance, legal, or financial advice. Coverage names and legal requirements vary by location. Confirm details with a licensed insurer or broker in your area.

1. Compare equivalent coverage

When reviewing quotes, check that limits, coverage types, deductibles, listed drivers, vehicle details, and usage are comparable. A lower premium may reflect less coverage or different assumptions.

2. Understand deductibles

A deductible is the amount you may have to pay toward a covered claim before insurance pays the remainder, subject to policy terms. A higher deductible can affect your premium, but it also means you may need more money available after a claim.

3. Ask what is included—and excluded

Read the policy summary and ask about exclusions, optional protections, rental-car coverage, roadside assistance, and how claims are handled. The right options depend on your situation and local rules.

4. Check discounts and eligibility

Ask providers which discounts may apply and what documentation is required. Never assume a discount is guaranteed until the insurer confirms eligibility.

5. Compare service as well as price

Consider how the insurer handles claims, what support is available, payment options, and how policy changes are managed. Review the full quote and policy documents before choosing.

6. Keep your information accurate

Use correct information about the vehicle, address, drivers, mileage, and intended use. Inaccurate details can affect eligibility, pricing, or coverage.
